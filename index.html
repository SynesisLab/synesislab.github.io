Why local-first

Local-first — No telemetry, no accounts, no background calls. Where cloud endpoints are supported they are strictly opt-in — Ternion ships a one-toggle local-only mode.

Own the stack — No subscriptions, no vendor lock-in. MIT licensed and self-hosted — fork it, modify it, run it yourself.

Bilingual by default — Every project ships with full English and Traditional Chinese support.

About

SynesisLab is an independent, open-source software lab founded in 2026 by Anthony Chen. Synesis (σύνεσις) is ancient Greek for “understanding” — the act of putting things together. The mission is the same: capable AI, with ownership and privacy, one self-hosted tool at a time.
